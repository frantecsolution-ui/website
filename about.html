About Franktec – AD Solutions

High-quality, tailor-made services for corporate and institutional clients—with professionalism and care for people and the environment.

Company overview

Franktec – AD Solutions is a Ghanaian owned business established as a full-service marketing communications company. We bridge the gap between our clients’ products and their customers by adopting the most appropriate, effective, efficient, and affordable channels to market those products.

We specialise in graphics designing, publications, T-shirt printing, secretarial services, clothes designing and printing, general printing, commercial videos for advertisement, binding, and branding. Our aim is to partner with your organisation to enhance brand identity, streamline communication, and boost market presence.

Vision

Regional leadership

To be the most creative, professional, and trusted integrated marketing communications company in West and Southern Africa—helping clients build strong, enduring brands. We deliver consistent, dynamic, and impactful solutions while upholding professionalism, protecting the environment and natural resources we use, and creating lasting value for the communities where we operate.

Mission

Value to life and business

To add value through quality, innovative, and superior services. We strive for world-class, efficient, and cost-effective marketing communication that meets diverse business challenges—so clients engage effectively with their markets and stay competitive locally and internationally.
